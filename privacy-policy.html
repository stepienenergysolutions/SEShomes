Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect customer information.

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Overview

SES Custom Homes respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you contact us, request an estimate, communicate with our team, or use our website.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, phone number, email address, and mailing or project address.
  • Project details, service interests, photos, notes, measurements, appointment details, and estimate requests.
  • Communication history, including calls, emails, text messages, form submissions, and customer service requests.
  • Basic website usage information, such as pages visited, browser type, device type, and referral information.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Respond to estimate requests and customer questions.
  • Schedule appointments, consultations, measurements, installations, and follow-ups.
  • Provide project updates, service information, invoices, payment links, and customer support.
  • Improve our website, services, internal operations, and customer experience.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, safety, and business record obligations.

Text Messaging and Phone Communications

Providing a mobile phone number does not by itself enroll you in text messages. If you actively opt in by checking the separate, unchecked SMS consent box on our estimate request form, you consent to receive recurring text messages from SES Custom Homes about your estimate request, appointments, scheduling, project updates, service follow-ups, invoices, payment links, and customer support. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact us at (804) 408-4663.

How We Share Information

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our website, CRM, scheduling, communications, payment processing, project management, and customer service tools. These providers are only authorized to use information as needed to provide services to us.

SES Custom Homes does not sell or rent personal information. SMS consent and mobile phone numbers will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We keep information for as long as needed to provide services, manage customer relationships, meet business needs, and comply with applicable obligations.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request that we update, correct, or delete your information, subject to records we need to keep for legitimate business or legal reasons. You may also opt out of text messages by replying STOP.
