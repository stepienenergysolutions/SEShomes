Privacy, Security & Style: Choosing the Right Fence for Your Virginia Property

May 3, 2026 • 6 minute read

Fencing is one of the most practical and impactful upgrades you can make to your Virginia property. It provides privacy, security, and curb appeal all at once. But with so many materials available, how do you choose the right one?

The Three Main Fence Types We Install

1. Vinyl Fencing

Vinyl is the most popular choice for good reason. It never needs painting, won’t rot or warp in Virginia’s humid climate, and requires almost zero maintenance. It comes in many styles and colors and offers excellent privacy.

2. Aluminum Fencing

Aluminum fencing is lightweight, extremely durable, and has a clean, modern look. It’s great for decorative front yards or pool areas. It offers good security while still allowing visibility.

3. Wood Fencing

Wood gives you that classic, natural look many homeowners love. Cedar and pressure-treated pine are the most common choices. Wood requires more maintenance (staining every few years) but can look beautiful when properly cared for.

Our Recommendation for Virginia Homes

For most families in Virginia, we recommend **vinyl fencing** as the best overall choice. It combines low maintenance, durability, and great privacy at a reasonable price. Aluminum is excellent for decorative or pool fencing, and wood is ideal if you want that traditional aesthetic and are willing to maintain it.

Real Client Story

A recent client in Hanover replaced their old wood fence with vinyl privacy fencing. “We love how it looks and that we don’t have to worry about painting or rotting anymore. It was worth every penny,” they said.
