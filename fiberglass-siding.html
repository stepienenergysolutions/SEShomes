Frequently Asked Questions About Fiberglass Siding

Is fiberglass siding better than vinyl?

Yes for homeowners who want a more premium wood-like look and greater durability and insulation.

How much does fiberglass siding cost in Virginia?

Installed prices typically range from $9 to $16 per square foot depending on style and color.

Does fiberglass siding require painting?

No. It comes pre-finished and maintains its color for many years.
