Frequently Asked Questions About Bay Windows

What is the difference between a bay window and a bow window?

Bay windows have angled sides with a flat front. Bow windows have a gentle curved shape with multiple smaller panes.

How much do bay windows cost in Virginia?

Installed prices typically range from $2,800 to $8,500+ depending on size, style, and glass options. Free estimates are always provided.

Are bay windows energy efficient?

Yes — when built with modern Low-E glass and insulated frames, they perform very well in Virginia’s climate.
