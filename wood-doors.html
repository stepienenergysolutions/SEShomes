Frequently Asked Questions About Wood Doors

Do wood doors require a lot of maintenance?

They do require occasional staining or painting to protect them from the elements, but high-quality finishes can last many years.

How much do wood doors cost in Virginia?

Installed prices typically range from $2,500 to $6,500+ depending on wood species, design, and glass options.

Are wood doors energy efficient?

Yes — when properly sealed and paired with modern insulated glass, they perform very well.
