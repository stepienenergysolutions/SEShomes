Wood vs Composite Decks: Which One Is Right for Your Virginia Backyard?

May 15, 2026 • 7 minute read

One of the most common questions we get from Virginia homeowners is: “Should I build a wood deck or a composite deck?” Both options have strong advantages, but they serve different needs. Here’s a clear, practical comparison based on hundreds of decks we’ve built across Virginia, West Virginia, and North Carolina.

Wood Decks

Wood decks offer unmatched natural beauty and warmth. Pressure-treated pine or cedar gives you that classic look many homeowners love. They are generally less expensive upfront and can be stained or painted to match your home perfectly.

Composite Decks

Composite decks are made from a blend of recycled wood fibers and plastic. They are engineered to resist fading, staining, splintering, and insect damage. They require almost zero maintenance — no annual sealing or staining needed.

Head-to-Head Comparison for Virginia Homes

Upfront Cost: Wood is cheaper initially
Maintenance: Composite wins by a mile (almost none)
Durability: Composite lasts longer with far less upkeep
Appearance: Wood feels more natural; modern composite looks very realistic
Comfort in Summer: Composite stays cooler underfoot

Our Recommendation for Virginia

If you want the lowest possible upfront cost and don’t mind annual maintenance, go with a high-quality wood deck. If you want a beautiful deck that stays looking new for decades with almost zero work, composite is the smarter long-term investment for most Virginia families.

Real Client Experience

One of our recent clients in Glen Allen chose composite after years of struggling with a wood deck. “We used to spend every spring sanding and staining. Now we just enjoy the deck. Best decision we ever made.”
