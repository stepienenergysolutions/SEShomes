Frequently Asked Questions About Composite Decks

How long do composite decks last?

High-quality composite decks typically last 25–50+ years with very little maintenance.

Are composite decks more expensive than wood?

Higher upfront cost, but much lower long-term maintenance and replacement costs.

Do composite decks get hot in the sun?

Modern composite materials are engineered to stay cooler than traditional wood or older composites.
