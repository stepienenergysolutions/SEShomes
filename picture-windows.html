Picture windows are large, fixed glass panes that create dramatic views and bring abundant natural light into any room. Unlike operable windows, picture windows are designed purely for beauty and daylight. At SES Custom Homes, we specialize in beautiful, energy-efficient picture windows that perfectly complement Virginia homes — whether you’re building new or replacing old windows.

Why Homeowners Choose Picture Windows

Maximum Natural Light

Unobstructed glass lets in significantly more daylight than standard windows, brightening your home and reducing electricity use.

Modern Curb Appeal

Large picture windows give your home a sleek, contemporary look that stands out in Virginia neighborhoods.

Energy Efficiency

High-performance Low-E glass and advanced framing keep your home comfortable year-round while lowering energy bills.

Quiet & Low Maintenance

No moving parts means fewer repairs and excellent sound insulation from outside noise.

Frequently Asked Questions About Picture Windows

What are picture windows best used for?

Living rooms, dining rooms, and great rooms where you want maximum views and natural light without needing ventilation.

How much do picture windows cost in Virginia?

Installed prices typically range from $850 to $2,200 depending on size and glass options. We provide free custom quotes.

Are picture windows energy efficient?

Yes. When built with today’s high-performance glass, they are among the most energy-efficient windows available.
