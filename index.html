Our Journey

Family Owned.
Built On Hard Work.

SES Custom Homes began in 2013 with a simple mission: helping homeowners improve the spaces they value most.

Starting in the window industry created the foundation for everything we are today. Working directly with families taught us what matters most: quality craftsmanship, honest service, and treating every project like it belongs to our own family.

As experience grew, so did the vision. From windows and exterior projects to complete renovations and major home improvements, every project strengthened our passion for building.

In 2020, we achieved a major milestone by earning our Class A Contractor License, transforming years of hands-on experience into a full-service custom home company.

Today, SES Custom Homes specializes in building personalized dream homes designed around the families who live inside them. From custom layouts and additions to premium finishes and forever homes, every project is built with integrity, craftsmanship, and pride.
