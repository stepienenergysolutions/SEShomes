Our journey began in 2013, starting in the window industry and learning construction from the ground up. Those early years shaped the way we still work today: show up prepared, explain every option clearly, and never treat a homeowner like just another job on the schedule.

Working directly with homeowners taught us the importance of honesty, craftsmanship, communication, and treating every project like it belongs to our own family. We saw how much trust it takes to invite a contractor into your home, and we built SES around earning that trust before, during, and after the work is complete.

In 2020, we earned our Class A Contractor License, transforming years of hands-on experience into a full-service custom home company. That opened the door for larger renovations, additions, exterior transformations, and complete custom builds while keeping the same personal, family-owned approach.

Today, SES Custom Homes builds custom dream homes around the families who live inside them — forever homes designed to last for generations. Whether we are replacing windows, adding outdoor living space, or building from the ground up, our goal is always the same: deliver work that feels thoughtful, durable, and deeply personal.
