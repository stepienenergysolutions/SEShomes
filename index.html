Our Journey

Built From Hard Work.
Built For Families.

Our journey began in 2013, starting in the window industry and learning construction from the ground up.

Working directly with homeowners taught us the importance of honesty, craftsmanship, communication, and treating every project like it belongs to our own family.

As experience grew, so did the vision. What began with windows expanded into larger renovations, additions, roofing, outdoor living spaces, and complete home transformations.

In 2020, we earned our Class A Contractor License, transforming years of hands-on experience into a full-service custom home company.

Today, SES Custom Homes specializes in custom dream homes built around the families who live inside them — creating forever homes designed to last for generations.
