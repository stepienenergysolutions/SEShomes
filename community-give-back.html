Community Give Back

$1 From Every Window Helps Local Families

Since 2013, SES Custom Homes has proudly donated $1 from every window we install to local non-profit organizations that make a real difference in Virginia, West Virginia, and North Carolina.

How Our Give Back Program Works

For every single window we install — whether it’s one replacement or an entire home — we donate $1 to our non-profit partners. No minimums. No limits. Every window makes an impact.

Our Non-Profit Partners

Habitat for Humanity

Building safe, affordable homes for families in Central Virginia.

Rebuilding Together

Providing critical home repairs for low-income seniors and veterans.

Virginia Energy Efficiency Alliance

Helping families lower energy costs through efficient home improvements.

Making a Real Difference Together

Since launching this program, we’ve donated thousands of dollars to local organizations. Every window you choose with us directly helps families stay in their homes, veterans receive repairs, and communities become stronger.
