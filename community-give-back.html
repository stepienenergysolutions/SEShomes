Community Give Back

$1 From Every Window Helps Local Families

Since 2013, SES Custom Homes has proudly donated $1 from every window we install to local non-profit organizations that make a real difference in Virginia, West Virginia, and North Carolina.

How Our Give Back Program Works

For every window we install — whether it’s a single replacement or a full home project — we donate $1 to our non-profit partners. It’s our way of giving back to the communities that support us.

Our Non-Profit Partners

Habitat for Humanity

Helping families build safe, affordable homes in Central Virginia.

Rebuilding Together

Providing critical home repairs for low-income seniors and veterans.

Virginia Energy Efficiency Alliance

Promoting energy-efficient homes and helping families reduce utility costs.

Making a Real Difference

Since launching our Give Back Program, we’ve donated thousands of dollars to local organizations — helping families stay in their homes, veterans receive repairs, and communities become stronger. Every window you choose helps make that possible.
