Home Additions: How to Grow Your Home Without Moving

May 8, 2026 • 7 minute read

Moving is expensive and stressful. That’s why more Virginia homeowners are choosing to add on to their current home instead. A well-designed home addition can give you the extra space you need while increasing your property value and letting you stay in the neighborhood you love.

Popular Types of Home Additions in Virginia

The most common additions we build are sunrooms, first-floor primary suites, home offices, and family room expansions. Sunrooms are especially popular because they let you enjoy the outdoors year-round while staying protected from bugs and weather.

Benefits of Adding On Instead of Moving

You keep your current location, schools, and neighbors. You avoid the high cost and hassle of buying and selling a home. And a well-planned addition often increases your home’s value by more than the cost of the project.

What to Consider Before Starting

Key factors include your local zoning rules, how the addition will connect to your existing home, energy efficiency, and matching the architectural style so it looks like it has always been there.

Real Client Story

One of our recent clients in Glen Allen added a 400 sq ft sunroom and home office. “We love our house and didn’t want to move. The addition feels like it was always part of the home. It’s now our favorite space,” they told us.
