Screened Porches vs Covered Porches: Which One Should You Build in Virginia?

May 10, 2026 • 6 minute read

Adding a porch is one of the most popular home improvement projects in Virginia. It instantly expands your living space and increases your home’s value. But the big question is: Should you build a **screened porch** or a **covered porch**?

Screened Porches

Screened porches give you the ability to enjoy the outdoors without bugs. They’re perfect for dining, relaxing, or just sitting outside during Virginia’s buggy summer evenings. You can add fans, lighting, and even outdoor furniture to make it feel like an extra room.

Covered Porches

Covered porches (without screens) are more open and airy. They’re great if you want maximum views and fresh air, but you’ll have to deal with bugs and rain. Many homeowners add retractable screens later if they change their mind.

Which One Is Better for Virginia?

For most Virginia homeowners, we recommend a **screened porch**. Our humid summers bring lots of mosquitoes, and a screened porch lets you actually use the space comfortably from spring through fall. Covered porches are better if you want a very open feel or already have good bug control.

Real Client Story

One of our recent clients in Mechanicsville built a large screened porch. “We use it almost every evening from April to October. It’s become our favorite room in the house,” they said.
