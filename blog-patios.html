Paver Patios vs Concrete Patios: Which Is Better for Virginia Homes?

May 5, 2026 • 6 minute read

Adding a patio is one of the smartest ways to increase your outdoor living space and home value. But when it comes to materials, the two most popular choices in Virginia are paver patios and concrete patios. Which one is better for your backyard?

Paver Patios

Pavers are individual concrete or stone blocks installed on a sand and gravel base. They offer incredible design flexibility — you can create patterns, curves, and custom borders. If one paver cracks or shifts, it can be easily replaced without tearing up the whole patio.

Concrete Patios

Concrete is poured as one solid slab. It’s usually cheaper upfront and can be stamped, stained, or colored to look like stone or brick. However, if it cracks (which is common in Virginia’s freeze-thaw cycles), the repair is more difficult and often noticeable.

Head-to-Head Comparison for Virginia

Cost: Concrete is usually cheaper initially
Maintenance: Pavers win (easier to repair)
Durability in Virginia Weather: Pavers perform better with freeze-thaw cycles
Design Flexibility: Pavers are far more versatile
Longevity: Both can last 20–30+ years with good installation

Our Recommendation

For most Virginia homeowners, we recommend **paver patios**. They handle our climate better, look more premium, and are much easier to repair if something shifts over time. Concrete is a good budget option if you want something simple and fast.

Real Client Story

A recent client in Short Pump chose a paver patio after their old concrete one cracked. “The pavers look beautiful and we’re confident they’ll last for decades with almost no maintenance,” they said.
