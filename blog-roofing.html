How to Choose the Right Roofing Material for Virginia Homes

May 12, 2026 • 7 minute read

Roofing is one of the biggest investments you’ll make in your home. In Virginia, where we face hot summers, cold winters, heavy rain, and occasional hail, choosing the right material is critical for both performance and longevity.

The Most Popular Roofing Options in Virginia

1. Asphalt Shingle Roofing
The most common and affordable choice. Modern architectural shingles offer excellent durability, a wide variety of colors, and good wind resistance. They typically last 20–30 years with proper installation.

2. Metal Roofing
Gaining popularity fast in Virginia. Metal roofs last 40–70+ years, reflect heat to keep your home cooler in summer, and handle snow and heavy rain extremely well. They also look very modern and sleek.

3. Flat Roofing (TPO, EPDM, Modified Bitumen)
Common on additions, sunrooms, and commercial-style homes. Modern flat roofing systems are far more reliable than they used to be and work very well when properly installed.

Our Recommendation for Virginia Homes

For most standard homes in Virginia, we recommend **architectural asphalt shingles** as the best balance of cost, performance, and appearance. However, if you want the longest lifespan and lowest long-term maintenance, **metal roofing** is an excellent upgrade that many of our clients are choosing in 2026.
