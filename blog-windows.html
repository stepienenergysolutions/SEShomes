Why Double Hung Windows Are Still the Most Popular Choice in Virginia

May 22, 2026 • 8 minute read

Double hung windows have been a staple in American homes for over 150 years — and in 2026 they remain the most requested window style by Virginia homeowners. But why? In a world full of modern designs like casement, slider, and picture windows, what keeps this classic design at the top?

The Perfect Balance of Tradition and Performance

The double hung window’s signature design features two independently operating sashes. This gives homeowners the ability to open just the top sash for ventilation while keeping the bottom closed for security, or open both for maximum airflow. This flexibility is especially valuable in Virginia’s hot, humid summers and cool winters.

Easy Cleaning — No Ladders Required

One of the biggest practical advantages is the tilt-in sash feature. Both the upper and lower sashes tilt inward, allowing you to clean the exterior glass safely from inside your home. This is a huge benefit for two-story homes common across Virginia.

Superior Energy Efficiency for Virginia Climate

Modern double hung windows from leading manufacturers now come with Low-E glass, argon gas fills, and advanced weatherstripping. Many of our clients in Richmond, Mechanicsville, and Glen Allen report noticeable drops in their heating and cooling bills after installation.

Timeless Curb Appeal

Unlike some contemporary styles that can look dated over time, double hung windows maintain a classic, elegant appearance that blends beautifully with both traditional Colonial and modern homes throughout Virginia.

Real Client Results

One recent client in Hanover replaced 22 old single-hung windows with premium double hung units. “The house feels brighter, quieter, and our energy bills dropped almost immediately,” they shared. “Plus, cleaning is so much easier now.”

Common Questions We Hear

Are double hung windows energy efficient? Yes — especially with today’s high-performance glass.

Do they work well in high wind areas? Absolutely, when properly installed with the right hardware.

How long do they last? With quality materials and proper installation, 25–40+ years is very common.

Is a Double Hung Window Right for Your Home?

If you value classic style, easy maintenance, excellent ventilation, and strong energy performance, double hung windows are often the smartest choice for Virginia homes.
