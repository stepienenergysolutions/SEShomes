Hopper windows are hinged at the bottom and tilt inward from the top, making them ideal for basements, bathrooms, and any area where you want controlled ventilation without letting rain in. They are highly popular in Virginia homes because they provide excellent airflow while protecting against our frequent rain showers. At SES Custom Homes, we install premium hopper windows that combine functionality, energy efficiency, and clean modern styling.

Why Homeowners Choose Hopper Windows

Rain-Resistant Ventilation

The inward tilt allows fresh air in while the overhang keeps rain out — perfect for Virginia weather.

Ideal for Basements & Bathrooms

Great for lower-level rooms where traditional windows are difficult to open outward.

Energy Efficient

Tight seals and modern glass keep your home comfortable and lower energy costs.

Easy to Clean & Maintain

Simple design with minimal moving parts means less maintenance over time.

Frequently Asked Questions About Hopper Windows

What are hopper windows best used for?

Basements, bathrooms, laundry rooms, and any area where you want ventilation without risking water damage.

How much do hopper windows cost in Virginia?

Installed prices typically range from $650 to $1,550 per window depending on size and glass options.

Do hopper windows open outward or inward?

They tilt inward from the top, so the glass swings into the room for easy cleaning and ventilation.
