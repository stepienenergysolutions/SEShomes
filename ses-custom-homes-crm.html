SES Custom Homes CRM

Our private business application for managing advertising performance, lead attribution, appointments, and customer follow-up.

Purpose of the application

SES Custom Homes CRM is an internal tool developed and operated by SES Custom Homes for authorized employees. It brings together marketing reporting, estimate requests, lead sources, appointments, communications, and sales outcomes so our team can respond to homeowners quickly and understand which marketing efforts produce qualified projects.

How we use the Google Ads API

The application connects to Google Ads accounts owned or managed by SES Custom Homes. We use the Google Ads API to retrieve campaign, advertising, cost, click, and conversion performance for internal reporting; connect leads and completed appointments with the campaigns that generated them; and send authorized conversion outcomes back to Google Ads so campaign performance can be measured and improved.

Google Ads data is used only for SES Custom Homes business operations. The application is not offered to the general public or to outside advertising clients, and Google Ads data is not sold.

Access and data protection

Access is limited to authorized SES Custom Homes personnel and service accounts used to operate the application. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards and retain information only as needed for reporting, customer service, business operations, and applicable legal obligations.
