Slider windows (also called sliding windows) glide horizontally on a track, making them ideal for large openings and modern homes. They are extremely popular for patios, sunrooms, and rooms where you want easy access to the outdoors without the swing space required by casement windows. At SES Custom Homes, we install high-performance slider windows that combine smooth operation, excellent energy efficiency, and contemporary style.

Why Homeowners Choose Slider Windows

Frequently Asked Questions About Slider Windows

Are slider windows good for energy efficiency?

Yes — modern slider windows with Low-E glass and strong weatherstripping perform very well in Virginia’s climate.

How much do slider windows cost in Virginia?

Installed prices typically range from $850 to $2,500+ per window depending on size and features.

What’s the difference between a slider window and a sliding patio door?

Slider windows are smaller and usually installed in walls. Sliding patio doors are much larger and designed as the main entry to a deck or patio.
