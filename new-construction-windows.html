Frequently Asked Questions About New Construction Windows

What’s the difference between new construction and replacement windows?

New construction windows are installed during the building process with a nailing flange. Replacement windows are designed to fit inside existing openings.

When should I choose new construction windows?

Any time you are building a new home, addition, or doing a full renovation that involves removing the old window frame.

Are new construction windows more energy efficient?

Yes — they typically offer better overall performance because they integrate directly with the wall structure.
