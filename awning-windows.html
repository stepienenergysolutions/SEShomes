Awning windows are hinged at the top and open outward from the bottom, creating an awning-like overhang. This design is excellent for ventilation while keeping rain out — making them very popular in Virginia’s unpredictable weather. At SES Custom Homes, we install high-quality awning windows that combine stylish looks with outstanding performance and durability.

Why Homeowners Choose Awning Windows

Rain-Resistant Ventilation

The top-hinged design lets you keep windows open during light rain without water entering your home.

Excellent Airflow

They direct fresh air upward and inward, providing great cross-ventilation.

Modern & Clean Look

The sleek, angled opening adds a contemporary architectural touch.

Easy to Operate & Maintain

Crank operation is smooth and they require very little maintenance.

Frequently Asked Questions About Awning Windows

Can awning windows be left open in the rain?

Yes — their top-hinged design acts like an awning, allowing ventilation while keeping most rain out.

How much do awning windows cost in Virginia?

Installed prices typically range from $700 to $1,650 per window depending on size and glass options.

Are awning windows energy efficient?

Yes, especially when paired with modern Low-E glass and strong weather sealing.
