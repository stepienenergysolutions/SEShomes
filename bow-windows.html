Frequently Asked Questions About Bow Windows

What is the difference between a bay window and a bow window?

Bay windows have straight, angled sides. Bow windows have a smooth, continuous curved shape with more glass panes.

How much do bow windows cost in Virginia?

Installed prices typically range from $3,200 to $9,500+ depending on size, number of panes, and glass options.

Are bow windows energy efficient?

Yes — especially with modern Low-E glass, argon fill, and well-insulated frames.
