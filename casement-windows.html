Casement Windows That Open Wide & Let Fresh Air In

Premium crank-out casement windows custom-built and installed by SES Custom Homes across Virginia, West Virginia, and North Carolina since 2013.

Casement windows are hinged on the side and open outward like a door, giving you excellent ventilation and a wide-open view. They are one of the most energy-efficient window styles available and are very popular in Virginia homes because they allow maximum airflow during our hot, humid summers. At SES Custom Homes, we install high-performance casement windows that combine sleek modern looks with outstanding durability and energy savings.

Why Homeowners Love Casement Windows

Maximum Ventilation

They open fully, allowing fresh air to flow freely — far more than double-hung or slider windows.

Superior Energy Efficiency

Tight seals and modern glass keep your home comfortable year-round and lower energy bills.

Unobstructed Views

No center mullion when fully open — just beautiful, clear views of the outdoors.

Easy Operation

Smooth crank operation makes them effortless to open and close, even on large windows.

Frequently Asked Questions About Casement Windows

Are casement windows good for energy efficiency?

Yes — casement windows are among the most energy-efficient styles because they seal very tightly when closed.

How much do casement windows cost in Virginia?

Installed prices typically range from $750 to $1,800 per window depending on size and options. We provide free estimates.

Can casement windows be used in high wind areas?

Yes — we offer impact-rated and reinforced casement windows designed for Virginia’s weather conditions.
